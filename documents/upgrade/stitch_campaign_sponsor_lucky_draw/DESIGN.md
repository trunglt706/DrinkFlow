## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, cryptographic-grade audit and transparency environment for enterprise and community sponsor raffles. The interface conveys uncompromising integrity, algorithmic speed, and cryptographic verifiability. Its aesthetic balances precision engineering with vibrant, electric momentum.

The design movement synthesizes **High-Tech Futurism** and **Tactile Glass-Brutalism**:
- Deep, obsidian voids punctuated by radiant emerald bioluminescence.
- Razor-sharp structural discipline paired with delicate, glowing translucent skins.
- Real-time telemetry cues: live cryptographic seed displays, deterministic entropy generation bars, and neon status conduits that make automated fairness visually palpable.
- Emotional resonance: absolute trust, technical mastery, exhilaration, and transparency.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an ultra-deep, cold-cast carbon core elevated by intense, high-luminance neon emerald and mint vectors.

### Core Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#0b1117`)**: The deep void canvas baseline providing maximal contrast for radiant elements.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#121b24`)**: Primary structural panels, tables, and inspection bays.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#18232e`)**: Active cards, dialog overlays, and interactive modules.
- **Primary Accent (`#00e676`)**: High-energy kinetic neon emerald. Reserved for primary calls-to-action, active raffle winning states, validated hash chains, and primary focus boundaries.
- **Secondary Accent (`#10b981`)**: Grounded deep emerald. Used for secondary active elements, structural fills, and stable status indications.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#00ffd5`)**: Pure cyan-mint conduit. Applied to real-time cryptographic pulse lines, entropy timers, and dynamic telemetry markers.
- **Surface Stroke & Dividers (`#1e2d3b`)**: Base boundary definition to preserve structure before glow activation.

### Accent & Glow Mechanics
- Neon emission states utilize alpha-layered box shadows with `#00e676` ranging from `15%` ambient scatter to `60%` focal beam intensities.
- Inactive data points utilize muted slate neutrals (`#64748b` down to `#334155`), ensuring emerald signals hold absolute visual priority.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy blends technical precision with modern brutalist clarity across three dedicated type engines:

1. **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides an angular, hyper-modern aesthetic for tier titles, total prize pools, and section anchors. Kerning is pulled tight to command structural presence.
2. **Body Copy & Contextual Overviews (`Geist`)**: Delivers ultra-clean neutral legibility against dark surfaces, engineered to prevent optical fatigue during intense audit reviews.
3. **Labels, Keys & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Handles all verifiable hash strings, seed numbers, participant counts, cryptographic blocks, and raffle tier badges. Letter-spacing is intentionally relaxed on smaller variants to ensure zero ambiguity between characters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a rigorous 12-column fluid grid system tailored for data density, verifiable ledger inspection, and sponsor presentation bays.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1rem), `gutter-sm` (1rem). Single-tier vertical flow where transaction tables and raffle chambers collapse into responsive stacked cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8 columns, `gutter` (1.5rem), `margin` (2rem). Two-column modular arrangement allowing live draw views adjacent to entry telemetry.
- **Desktop (>1024px)**: 12 columns, max bounded width `1440px`, `gutter-lg` (2rem), `margin-desktop` (3rem). Expansive multi-panel command cockpit featuring persistent verification monitors, sponsor display showcases, and live ledger graphs.

### Spacing Cadence
- Grid-level distances rely exclusively on `gutter` and `margin` definitions.
- Internal component rhythm follows an exact 4px-scaled ladder (`space-xs` to `space-xl`) keeping inner card padding, telemetry gaps, and action clusters geometrically cohesive.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on traditional muddy black shadows; it is driven by **luminant ambient halos, high-contrast borders, and frosted translucent paneling**.

### Layer Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas `#0b1117`)**: Base viewport ground. Micro dot-grid or subtle vector trace lines sit at 4% opacity.
- **Level 1 (Docked Containers `#121b24`)**: Bordered with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Semi-translucent with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Modules `#18232e`)**: Bordered with `1px solid rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.2)`. Casts a soft ambient halo: `0 8px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 16px -2px rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Neon Active / Live Draw Focus)**: Bordered with `1px solid #00e676`. Active glow radiance: `0 0 24px 2px rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.35), inset 0 0 12px 0 rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.15)`.

### Glass & Light Behavior
Surfaces utilize layered transparency so live telemetry feeds and animated gradient beams gently refract through upper tiers, reinforcing the sensation of transparent, open-source execution.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a sharp, architectural **Soft (1)** shape language. The subtle radius softens clinical harshness while preserving the engineered, hardware-grade feel of precision equipment:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`)**: Buttons, badges, input controls, chips, and data-cell indicators.
- **Surface Radius (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Raffle overview cards, sponsor feature tiles, modal containers, and interactive telemetry charts.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-xl` / `0.75rem` / `12px`)**: Outer dashboard frames, master stage inspection chambers, and floating bottom navigation bars.
- Specialized indicators (status orbs, live pulse blips, circular progress gauges) retain native `rounded-full` form factors for unambiguous focal contrast.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic**: Background `#00e676`, foreground text `#0b1117`, font `JetBrains Mono` bold uppercase. On hover, expands an outer neon emerald glow `0 0 20px rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.5)` with micro scale transform (`1.02x`).
- **Secondary Ghost**: Background `rgba(18, 27, 36, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.4)`, text `#00e676`. Hover triggers background saturation to `#121b24` and full border opacity.
- **Destructive / Abort**: Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)`, border `1px solid #ef4444`, text `#ef4444`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips**: Monospaced micro-labels with leading pulsing dot (e.g., `[LIVE SEED]`, `[VERIFIED]`, `[DRAWING]`). Encased in `rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.08)` fill with `1px solid rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.3)` perimeter.
- **Sponsor Tier Chips**: Metallic graphite backings with crisp emerald typography indicating sponsor equity weighting.

### Inputs & Verification Fields
- Dark canvas wells `#0b1117` inset with `1px solid #1e2d3b`. Placeholder text in muted slate (`#64748b`).
- **Focus State**: Transitions to `1px solid #00e676` accompanied by inner neon trace `inset 0 0 8px rgba(0, 230, 118, 0.2)`.

### Cards & Raffle Chambers
- Standard cards feature a dark background (`#121b24`) framed with a top-lit 1px subtle gradient border (`linear-gradient(90deg, #00e676, transparent 60%)`).
- **Active Winner Chambers**: Highlighted card envelope with an animated pulsing emerald halo, displaying deterministic random seeds, ticket ranges, and real-time ledger outputs.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Square (4px radius) or circular elements with an obsidian base and `1px solid #1e2d3b` border. When selected, fills with solid `#00e676`, featuring an obsidian high-contrast check or dot mark with ambient box radiance.

### Telemetry & Live Ledger Feed
- Chronological inspection feed displaying cryptographic hashes truncated with monospace clarity (`0x4f...9e2`).
- Each new entry triggers an emerald scanline wipe animation down the item row before settling into static high-legibility state.
